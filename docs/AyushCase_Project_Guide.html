1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

AyushCase is an intelligent clinical case-taking and decision-support web software engineered specifically for the Ministry of AYUSH under Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement: SIH26047). It bridges the critical gap between classical holistic diagnosis and modern digital healthcare standards by providing an automated, bidirectional ecosystem for patients and clinicians.

🚨 The Traditional Clinical Problem

  • Lengthy Case Taking: Classical AYUSH consultations take 35–45 minutes per patient to evaluate Ashtavidha Pariksha (8-fold pulse/tongue examination), Prakriti, and digestive fire.
  • Paper Prescriptions: Handwritten formulations, lost physical files, and unreadable classical scripts.
  • Lack of Dual Coding: Difficult to map Ayurvedic conditions to modern WHO ICD-11 diagnostic codes.
  • Herb-Drug Hazards: Patients taking allopathic blood-thinners or antidiabetics alongside herbal remedies without interaction checks.

✨ The AyushCase Solution

  • Pre-Consultation AI Interview: Gathers patient history before entering the doctor's cabin, saving up to 70% of consultation time.
  • Smart Record Digitization: Uploads handwritten AYUSH prescriptions and converts them into structured digital records via OCR.Space & AI.
  • 1-Click Case Import: Clinicians import verified medications directly into the official case sheet with exact Anupana and timing.
  • Dual Diagnostic Bridge: Side-by-side formulation of Ayurvedic Roga with modern ICD-11 terminology.

2. System Architecture & Workflows

The application is split into two tightly synchronized portals sharing a centralized SQLite/Prisma database:

1
Patient Portal (Self-Service Intake & Digitization):

Patient signs up with ABHA ID → Completes conversational AI Health Interview → Uploads physical prescriptions/lab reports → AI structures medicines with Anupana → Reviews in Lightbox preview → Receives instant Digital Health Passport & Print Slip.

2
Doctor Clinical Console & Case-Taking Engine:

Doctor opens patient profile → Views AI Pre-Consultation synthesis & OCR documents → Conducts Ashtavidha Pariksha → Selects Dual Ayurvedic + ICD-11 diagnosis → 1-Click imports digitized medications into Prescription Builder → Generates official Ministry-compliant clinical case sheet.

3
Multi-Agent AI Clinical Assistant (Ayush AI):

4 Specialized AI Personas (AyurVaidya, Clinical Pariksha, AyushGuard, AyushCare) powered by Groq Cloud AI with offline fallback.
